Ultrasound scans

A safe, painless scan that shows the inside of your body on a screen.

Ask the price on WhatsAppCall 0905 300 2875

What it is

An ultrasound scan uses sound waves to make pictures of the inside of the body. It does not use radiation, and it does not hurt.

We do scans of the tummy (liver, kidneys and other organs), the womb and ovaries, and other scans. Our full list: to confirm

Why people do it

  • To check a pregnancy and how the baby is growing.
  • To find the cause of pain or swelling in the tummy or lower belly.
  • To check organs such as the liver, kidneys, womb and ovaries.
  • When your doctor asks for a scan.

How it works at Acouns

  1. Message or call us to book, and tell us which scan you need.

  2. Lie on a couch. We put a little gel on your skin and move a small probe over it.

  3. Most scans take 15 to 30 minutes. to confirm

  4. You get the report. We tell you when it will be ready. to confirm

Before you come

  • Some scans, such as a pelvic scan, need a full bladder: drink water and do not pass urine before.
  • Some tummy scans need you to avoid food for a few hours.
  • We tell you exactly what to do when you book.

Questions

Is ultrasound safe in pregnancy?

Yes. It uses sound waves, not radiation, and it is used all through pregnancy.

Does it hurt?

No. You may feel a little pressure from the probe, and the gel feels cool.
