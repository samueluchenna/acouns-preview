What it is

Couples do a few simple blood tests before the wedding, so they know their health and can plan their family well.

They usually include genotype (AA, AS or SS), blood group and Rhesus factor, HIV, and hepatitis B and C. Our full list: to confirm

Why people do it

  • Genotype: if both partners are AS, each pregnancy has a 1 in 4 chance of a child with sickle cell disease (SS). Knowing early lets you plan with a doctor.
  • Rhesus factor: if the woman is Rhesus negative and the man is Rhesus positive, the doctor plans care in pregnancy to protect the baby.
  • Infections: some infections show no signs at all. Testing protects both of you and your future children.
  • Many churches, and some mosques, ask for these results before the wedding.

How it works at Acouns

  1. Message or call us to book.

  • Bring the form from your church or mosque if they gave you one.
